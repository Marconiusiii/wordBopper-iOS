# WordBopper

WordBopper is a fast, bouncy word game for iPhone and iPad. Tap letter bubbles, make words, score points, and try to beat your best round across timed, Bopple, and non-stop play.

## How It Works

Choose a grid size, pick a bubble language, and tap letters to build a word. Use Make Word to score it, or Clear Letters/Clear Word to reset what you are building.

Timed mode gives you 2 minutes on the clock. Bopple mode keeps letters in place and uses Boggle-style scoring. Non-Stop mode removes the timer so you can keep bopping until you end the game.

Connected words earn bonus points in Timed and Non-Stop modes. Make enough connected words in a row and you unlock a short 3 times scoring boost.

## Features

- Timed, Bopple, and Non-Stop game modes
- Adjustable 3 by 3, 4 by 4, 5 by 5, and 6 by 6 grids
- Multiple bubble languages, including English, Spanish, French, German, Italian, and Brazilian Portuguese
- Letter-bopping word play
- Connected-word bonuses in Timed and Non-Stop modes
- Boggle-style Bopple scoring
- Optional BopAway challenge mode
- 3 times scoring boost
- Best-game tracking per mode for score, longest word, word count, and largest chain where applicable
- Original game sounds
- Optional game haptics
- Missing word reporting from the About screen

## Accessibility

WordBopper is built with VoiceOver play in mind. The game uses clear screen headings, large touch targets, spoken gameplay feedback, and an audio-forward design so the experience is playable without relying on sight.

The app supports portrait and landscape layouts with edge-to-edge touch regions to reduce dead space when exploring by touch. Gameplay and results controls are designed to stay reachable at screen edges.

Accessibility-focused settings include spoken letter positions, optional letter phonetics, bubble language pronunciation, game announcement verbosity, left-handed mode, bubble letter style, bubble text color, and optional haptics. The app also supports Dynamic Type and respects reduced-motion settings where animations are used.

Accessibility is part of the core game design, not an add-on.

## Word Lists

WordBopper includes built-in word lists for supported bubble languages. Word list credits and license acknowledgements are available in the app under Game Settings, About WordBopper, Acknowledgements.

## Privacy

WordBopper does not collect personal information, use analytics, show ads, track users, or send gameplay data anywhere.

Best-game statistics, settings, and gameplay preferences are stored locally on your device.
